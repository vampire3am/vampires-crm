# AECS Staff User Guide

## Daily workflow

1. Sign in with your staff email and password.
2. Register new students from **Students → Add student**.
3. Search and filter records in **Students**.
4. Open a profile to review details, edit information, change status, or print.
5. Record counselling notes and follow-up dates in **Counselling**.
6. Upload and review private student files in **Documents**.
7. Track university applications and visa progress in **Applications**.
8. Use **Dashboard** for current management totals and trends.

Only administrators and directors can delete records, change staff roles, or disable accounts.
